Headroom beside Tessera

Loads Tessera through webgllm.images (in webgllm's own worker), draws sprites, then fills the page's memory &block= MB at a time (64; touched, so it counts) until the browser kills the tab: the largest BALLAST line is the headroom left. ?stage= none (no model), loaded, n1 or n4 (after 1 or 4 sprites), unloaded (after 4 sprites and an unload). &webgpu=0 for WebGL 2. &once=<name> runs only while /models/<name> exists (see unload-headroom.html).
