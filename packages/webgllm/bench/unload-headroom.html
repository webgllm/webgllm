Headroom after an unload

Loads a model, unloads it one way or another, then fills the page's memory 16 MB at a time (touched, so it counts) until the browser kills the tab: the last BALLAST line is the headroom left (an iPhone's tab dies near 2 GB; with pace=0 the lines arrive out of order, so take the largest). ?arm= none (no model), none-spare (no model, an idle worker), loaded (filled with the model loaded), loaded-churn (that, after its worker made and dropped 512 MB of garbage), pool (webgllm's own worker, unloadModel()), own (a worker of the page's, unloadModel()), own-end (that, then the page ends its worker), own-churn (that, then the worker makes and drops 512 MB of garbage), own-spare (like own-end, with a worker started at the beginning for the reload), probe (no model: whether the worker's FinalizationRegistry and WeakRef report what its collector took; &mode=promote&keep=<n> keeps each round's garbage for n rounds). &wait=<s> waits after the unload. &reload=1 loads the model again after the unload and times it, instead of filling (in a new worker for own-end). &ballast=0 stops before filling. &pace=<ms> between blocks (100; 0 fills at once, as a model loading next does). &worker=<url> runs the page's workers from a prebuilt script (by default unload-worker.ts, bundled on each request). &model=, &ctx= (8192). &once=<name> runs only while /models/<name> exists (a driver can delete it once the fill starts, so a tab Safari reloads after the kill doesn't run again).
